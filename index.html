A little about me

I grew up in India, where I served in ministry before moving to the United States. Today I pastor a local church, serve ministries across several countries, and work in technology. I named this site Gospel-Link because the gospel is good news, and my hope is simply to link that good news with people.

Senior Pastor, MPA Church, Saint Paul Park, Minnesota
